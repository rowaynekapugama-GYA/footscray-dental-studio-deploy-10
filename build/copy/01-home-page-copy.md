## Why Choose Footscray Dental Studio

Our team brings more than 20 years of combined experience across general and comprehensive dental care. As part of our commitment to modern, patient-focused dentistry, our studio is equipped with:

- **Intraoral cameras** for detailed, real-time views of your teeth and gums
- **Digital X-rays** for efficient, high-quality diagnostic imaging
- **A digital dental scanner** for accurate, comfortable impressions without traditional putty moulds
- **OPG (panoramic) imaging** for a full view of teeth, jaws and surrounding structures
- **Advanced root canal treatment technology** to support precise, efficient endodontic care
- **Smile photography and digital documentation**, including a dedicated studio-style setup to track treatment progress

We take the time to listen, explain your options clearly, and help you make informed decisions about your care — with a warm, approachable team you can build a long-term relationship with.
