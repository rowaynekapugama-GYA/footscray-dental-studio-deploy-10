## What's Involved

A smile makeover consultation at our practice generally includes:

1. **In-depth consultation** — your dentist discusses your goals, concerns, and any previous dental history in detail.
2. **Comprehensive assessment** — including digital photography, X-rays, and a digital scan where appropriate, to build a complete picture of your teeth and bite.
3. **Treatment planning** — your dentist develops a coordinated plan, which may combine treatments such as whitening, veneers, bonding, crowns, or implants, depending on your individual needs.
4. **Staged treatment** — treatment is carried out in a logical sequence, which your dentist will explain clearly from the outset, including expected timeframes.
5. **Review** — your progress is reviewed throughout treatment, with adjustments made as needed.

## Who It's Suitable For

A smile makeover approach may suit patients considering multiple treatments, those with more complex cosmetic or functional concerns, or anyone who would prefer a single, coordinated plan rather than making decisions treatment-by-treatment. Your dentist will discuss whether this approach is appropriate for your situation, or whether a single, standalone treatment may better suit your needs and goals.

## What to Expect During the Process

Because a smile makeover can involve multiple treatments and visits, your dentist will provide a clear overview of the proposed plan, expected timeframe, and what each stage involves before you commit to proceeding. You'll have the opportunity to ask questions and adjust the plan as needed at each stage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's included in a smile makeover?**
This varies by patient — a smile makeover may combine treatments such as whitening, veneers, bonding, crowns or implants, depending on your individual goals and needs.

**How long does a smile makeover take?**
Timeframes vary significantly depending on the treatments involved — your dentist will provide an estimated timeline as part of your consultation.

**Do I need to commit to the full plan upfront?**
Your dentist will discuss the full proposed plan with you, but treatment can often be staged, allowing you to reassess as you go.

**Is a smile makeover suitable for functional issues, not just appearance?**
Yes, smile makeover planning can incorporate restorative treatments addressing function as well as appearance, depending on your individual needs.

**How much does a smile makeover cost?**
Cost depends entirely on the treatments involved in your individual plan — your dentist will provide a clear breakdown following your consultation.

**Can I see examples of what a smile makeover might achieve for me?**
In line with AHPRA advertising guidelines, we don't display patient before-and-after imagery. Your dentist can discuss realistic expectations for your specific situation during a consultation.
