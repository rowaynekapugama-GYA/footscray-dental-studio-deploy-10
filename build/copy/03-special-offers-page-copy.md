## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are these offers available to all patients?**
Most offers are available to new and existing patients; suitability for any treatment is confirmed at consultation with your dentist.

**Can I combine multiple offers?**
Please contact our team to confirm which offers can be used together, as this may vary depending on your treatment needs.

**Do offers expire?**
Offers are subject to change — please check with our team or this page for current availability before booking your appointment.

**Is the Child Dental Benefits Schedule the same as a free offer?**
No — CDBS is a government scheme, not a practice promotion. Eligible children can access bulk-billed services; contact us to check eligibility for your child.

**Is teeth whitening suitable for everyone?**
Suitability is assessed individually at consultation, as whitening isn't appropriate for every patient or every tooth condition.

**How do I redeem an offer?**
Simply mention the offer when booking online or by phone, and our team will confirm the details with you ahead of your appointment.
