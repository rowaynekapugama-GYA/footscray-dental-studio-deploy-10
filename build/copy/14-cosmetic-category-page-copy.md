## H1: Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry covers a range of treatments designed to improve the appearance of your teeth and smile. At Footscray Dental Studio, every cosmetic treatment begins with a thorough consultation, where your dentist assesses your oral health, discusses your goals, and talks you through the options genuinely suited to your individual situation. Results vary from patient to patient, and your dentist will always give you a realistic picture of what a treatment can achieve before you proceed.

## What to Expect at Footscray Dental Studio

Cosmetic treatment at our practice starts with understanding what you'd like to achieve and why. Your dentist will examine your teeth and gums, discuss suitable options, and explain the process, timeframe and any considerations involved — so you can make an informed decision with no pressure to proceed on the day. Where multiple visits are required, your dentist will map out a clear plan from the outset.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which cosmetic treatment is right for me?**
This depends on your individual goals, the condition of your teeth, and your budget and timeframe — your dentist will discuss suitable options at a consultation.

**Are cosmetic treatments permanent?**
Longevity varies by treatment type and individual factors such as oral hygiene and habits — your dentist will explain what to expect for your chosen treatment.

**Do cosmetic treatments hurt?**
Most cosmetic treatments are minimally invasive or non-invasive; your dentist will explain what to expect for your specific treatment during your consultation.

**Can I combine multiple cosmetic treatments?**
Yes, many patients combine treatments such as whitening and veneers or bonding — this is best discussed as part of a smile makeover consultation.

**Will you show me before-and-after photos of past patients?**
In line with AHPRA advertising guidelines for regulated health services, we don't display patient before-and-after imagery on our website. We're happy to discuss what a treatment may involve for your specific situation during a consultation.

**How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?**
Cost depends on the treatment and your individual needs — your dentist will provide a clear quote following your consultation.
