## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I know which service I need?**
Book a consultation and our team will assess your individual needs before recommending a course of treatment tailored to you.

**Do you treat both adults and children?**
Yes, we provide general and preventive dental care for patients of all ages, including services covered under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule for eligible children.

**Are your services covered by health funds?**
We accept all major health funds, with on-the-spot claiming available through HICAPS. Coverage depends on your specific policy and treatment.

**Can I have more than one type of treatment in a single visit?**
This depends on the treatments involved and will be discussed with you during your consultation, based on your individual needs and preferences.

**Do I need a referral to book any of these services?**
No referral is needed for most services — simply book an appointment directly. Some specialist treatments may involve a referral where clinically appropriate.

**What if my treatment needs aren't listed here?**
Get in touch with our team — if we can't help directly, we're happy to point you in the right direction.
