## H1: Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing damaged, decayed or missing teeth to restore comfort, function and confidence. Whether you need a simple filling or a more involved treatment such as dental implants, our team takes an individualised approach — assessing your specific situation and explaining your options clearly before recommending a course of treatment.

## What to Expect at Footscray Dental Studio

Restorative treatment begins with a thorough assessment, often supported by digital X-rays or an OPG scan to understand the full picture of your oral health. Your dentist will explain what's involved, the expected timeframe, and any alternatives available, so you can make an informed decision about your care.

## Our Restorative Services

**[Fillings](/services/restorative/fillings/)**
Tooth-coloured composite fillings used to repair teeth affected by decay, restoring both function and appearance.

**[Crowns & Bridges](/services/restorative/crowns-bridges/)**
Custom-made restorations used to protect damaged teeth or replace one or more missing teeth.

**[Root Canal Therapy](/services/restorative/root-canal/)**
Treatment for infected or severely damaged tooth pulp, aimed at saving the natural tooth wherever possible.

**[Dental Implants](/services/restorative/dental-implants/)**
A long-term option for replacing one or more missing teeth, anchored directly into the jawbone.

**[All-on-X Dental Implants](/services/restorative/all-on-x-implants/)**
A full-arch implant solution for patients missing most or all of their teeth in an upper or lower jaw.

**[Dentures](/services/restorative/dentures/)**
Full or partial dentures designed to restore missing teeth and support everyday function.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I know which restorative treatment I need?**
This depends on your specific dental concern — your dentist will assess your teeth, often supported by X-rays, and recommend the most suitable option.

**Are restorative treatments covered by health funds?**
Many restorative treatments are covered to some extent by private health insurance — coverage depends on your individual policy.

**Is restorative treatment painful?**
Modern restorative dentistry, including the use of local anaesthetic where appropriate, is generally well tolerated — your dentist will explain what to expect for your specific treatment.

**Can I have sedation for restorative treatment?**
Please discuss sedation options with our team directly, as suitability depends on the treatment and your individual circumstances.

**How long do restorative treatments last?**
This varies by treatment type and individual factors such as oral hygiene — your dentist can discuss what to expect for your specific situation.

**Do I need a referral for implants or root canal therapy?**
No referral is generally needed to book a consultation — your dentist will advise if referral to a specialist is appropriate following assessment.
