Professional teeth whitening is one of the most straightforward ways to address surface and deeper staining, using controlled, dentist-supervised methods rather than generic over-the-counter products. ## What's Involved

Professional whitening at our practice generally involves:

1. **Consultation and assessment** — your dentist examines your teeth and gums to confirm you're a suitable candidate and discusses your goals.
2. **Shade assessment** — your current tooth shade is recorded so progress can be tracked.
3. **Treatment** — depending on the option recommended, whitening may be carried out in-chair, via a take-home kit, or a combination of both.
4. **Review** — your dentist checks the outcome and discusses any touch-up or maintenance options.

## Whitening Options

- **In-chair whitening** — a professionally applied treatment carried out at the practice under dentist supervision.
- **Take-home whitening** — custom-fitted trays and professional-strength whitening gel for use at home, following your dentist's instructions.

Your dentist will recommend the option best suited to your teeth, goals, and lifestyle.

## Who It's Suitable For

Teeth whitening is generally suitable for patients with staining or discolouration who are in good general oral health. It isn't suitable for everyone — for example, patients with certain existing dental work, untreated decay, or gum disease may need other issues addressed first. Whitening results can also vary between individuals depending on the type and cause of staining. Your dentist will confirm your suitability at consultation.

## What to Expect Afterwards

Some temporary sensitivity is common after whitening treatment. Your dentist will provide guidance on caring for your teeth afterwards, including any dietary recommendations to help maintain your results.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does teeth whitening take?**
This depends on the method used — in-chair whitening is typically completed in a single visit, while take-home whitening is used over a period at home as directed by your dentist.

**Is teeth whitening painful?**
Some patients experience temporary tooth sensitivity during or after treatment, which your dentist can help manage.

**How long do results last?**
This varies between individuals depending on diet, habits such as smoking, and oral hygiene — your dentist can discuss what to expect and how to help maintain your results.

**Is teeth whitening safe?**
Professional whitening carried out under dentist supervision is a widely used cosmetic treatment; your dentist will confirm suitability for your specific teeth and oral health.

**Will whitening work on crowns or veneers?**
No, whitening only affects natural tooth enamel — existing crowns, veneers or fillings won't change colour, which your dentist will discuss if relevant to your treatment plan.

**Can I choose how white my teeth become?**
Your dentist will discuss achievable and appropriate shade options with you as part of your consultation, based on your natural teeth and treatment type.
