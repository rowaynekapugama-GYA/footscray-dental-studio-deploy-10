## What's Involved

The process for a custom mouthguard or night guard typically includes:

1. **Assessment** — your dentist discusses your needs, whether for sport, teeth grinding, or both.
2. **Digital scan or impression** — a digital scan or physical impression of your teeth is taken to capture an accurate model of your mouth.
3. **Custom fabrication** — your mouthguard is made to fit your teeth precisely, using this model.
4. **Fitting** — your dentist checks the fit and comfort of the finished mouthguard and makes adjustments if needed.

## Sport Mouthguards

Custom sport mouthguards are recommended for patients, including children and teenagers, participating in contact or high-impact sports. A well-fitted mouthguard can be worn more comfortably and consistently than a generic option, which matters since protection is only effective when a mouthguard is actually worn during play.

## Night Guards for Teeth Grinding (Bruxism)

Night guards are recommended for patients experiencing teeth grinding or clenching, a condition known as bruxism. This often occurs during sleep and can contribute to worn or damaged teeth, jaw discomfort, and headaches over time. Your dentist can help identify signs of bruxism during a check-up and discuss whether a night guard is appropriate for you.

- Designed to accommodate your specific bite and dental structure
- Suitable for a wide range of sports and for managing teeth grinding

## Caring for Your Mouthguard

Your dentist will provide guidance on cleaning and storing your mouthguard to help it last, along with when it may need to be checked or replaced — particularly important for children whose mouths are still developing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are custom mouthguards better than store-bought ones?**
Custom mouthguards are made to fit your teeth precisely, generally offering better comfort and protection than generic, off-the-shelf alternatives, and are more likely to be worn consistently.

**How long does it take to get a custom mouthguard?**
Turnaround time can vary depending on fabrication — our team can confirm timing when you book your appointment.

**Do children need custom mouthguards for sport?**
Yes, children participating in contact sports can benefit from a custom-fitted mouthguard, and we can accommodate growing or changing teeth with periodic remakes.

**How do I know if I need a night guard?**
Signs of teeth grinding include jaw soreness, headaches, or visible tooth wear — your dentist can assess this during a check-up and discuss whether a night guard would help.

**How often should a mouthguard be replaced?**
This depends on wear, growth (for children), and changes to your teeth over time — your dentist will advise at your regular check-ups.

**Can I use the same mouthguard for multiple sports?**
In most cases, yes — let your dentist know what activities you need it for so they can advise on the most suitable design.
