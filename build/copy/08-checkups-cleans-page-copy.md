Regular dental check-ups and professional cleans form the foundation of good oral health. While daily brushing and flossing go a long way, they can't reach every surface of your teeth or fully remove hardened plaque (tartar) that builds up over time. A routine visit to Footscray Dental Studio allows your dentist to assess the health of your teeth, gums and mouth, catch early signs of decay or gum disease before they become bigger problems, and keep your smile in the best possible condition.

At Footscray Dental Studio, we combine thorough clinical examination with modern diagnostic technology, including intraoral cameras and digital X-rays where needed, so you have a clear picture of your oral health at every visit.

## What's Involved

A typical check-up and clean at our practice generally includes:

1. **Examination** — your dentist reviews your teeth, gums, tongue and soft tissue for signs of decay, gum disease, or other concerns.
2. **Diagnostic imaging (if needed)** — digital X-rays or intraoral camera images may be taken to support a thorough assessment, particularly for areas not visible to the naked eye.
3. **Professional clean (scale and polish)** — plaque and tartar build-up is carefully removed from your teeth, including areas that are difficult to reach with a toothbrush alone.
4. **Polishing** — your teeth are polished to remove surface staining and leave a smooth, clean finish.
5. **Discussion and planning** — your dentist talks you through any findings, answers your questions, and outlines next steps if further treatment is recommended.

## Benefits of Regular Check-ups & Cleans

- Early identification of decay, gum disease and other oral health issues
- Removal of plaque and tartar that home brushing can't fully address
- An opportunity to ask questions and get professional guidance on your oral care routine
- Reduced likelihood of needing more complex, costly treatment later
- Peace of mind knowing your oral health is being actively monitored

## Signs It May Be Time for a Visit

While we recommend regular check-ups regardless of symptoms, you should book a visit sooner if you notice:

- Bleeding or swollen gums
- Sensitivity to hot, cold or sweet foods
- Visible discolouration, chips or cracks
- Persistent bad breath
- Discomfort when chewing

## Who It's Suitable For

Check-ups and cleans are recommended for patients of all ages as part of routine oral healthcare. Most patients are advised to visit every six months, though your dentist may suggest a different frequency depending on your individual oral health, risk factors, and dental history.

## What to Expect During Your Visit

Your appointment will be relaxed and unhurried — our team takes the time to explain what we're doing and why, so you always know what to expect. If any additional treatment is recommended, we'll walk you through your options clearly before proceeding, with no pressure to decide on the spot.

## Caring for Your Teeth Between Visits

Maintaining good oral hygiene between appointments helps preserve the results of your professional clean and supports long-term oral health. This includes:

- Brushing twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- Daily flossing or use of interdental brushes
- Limiting sugary food and drinks
- Attending your recommended check-up schedule

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a check-up and clean take?**
Appointment length can vary depending on individual needs, but a standard check-up and clean is typically a straightforward, single visit. Our team can give you a more specific estimate when you book.

**Is a professional clean painful?**
Most patients find a professional clean comfortable. If you experience sensitivity, let your dentist know so they can adjust their approach and make you more comfortable.

**Will I need X-rays at every check-up?**
Not necessarily — your dentist will recommend X-rays based on your dental history, risk factors and individual needs, rather than as a routine part of every single visit.

**How often should children have check-ups?**
Children are generally recommended to have check-ups every six months, similar to adults, allowing your dentist to monitor their developing teeth and provide early guidance on oral hygiene habits.

**What happens if a problem is found during my check-up?**
Your dentist will explain any findings clearly, discuss the available options, and help you decide on next steps — there's no obligation to proceed with treatment on the same day.

**Can I book a check-up and clean as a new patient?**
Yes — see our New Patient Exam & Clean offer, or contact us directly to book your first visit.
