## H1: General & Preventive Dentistry

Good oral health starts with prevention. Our general dentistry services are focused on maintaining healthy teeth and gums, identifying potential issues early, and helping you avoid more complex — and often more costly — treatment down the track. We recommend a check-up and clean every six months for most patients, though your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your individual needs and risk factors.

At Footscray Dental Studio, our general and preventive services are supported by modern diagnostic technology, including digital X-rays, OPG imaging and intraoral cameras, giving your dentist a clear, detailed picture of your oral health at every visit.

## What to Expect at Footscray Dental Studio

Every general and preventive visit begins with a thorough assessment of your teeth, gums and overall oral health. ## Our General & Preventive Services

**[Check-ups & Cleans](/services/general-preventive/check-ups-cleans/)**
Routine examinations and professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, identify early signs of decay or gum disease, and keep your smile in good health. Recommended every six months for most patients.

**[Digital X-Rays / Diagnostics](/services/general-preventive/digital-xrays/)**
Detailed, low-radiation imaging — including bitewing, periapical and OPG panoramic X-rays — to support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

**[Fluoride Treatments](/services/general-preventive/fluoride-treatments/)**
Professional-strength fluoride application to help strengthen tooth enamel and reduce the risk of decay, suitable for both children and adults.

**[Fissure Sealants](/services/general-preventive/fissure-sealants/)**
A protective coating applied to the grooved chewing surfaces of back teeth, commonly recommended for children as their adult molars emerge.

**[Gum Disease / Periodontal Care](/services/general-preventive/gum-disease-care/)**
Assessment and treatment to support healthy gums, from early-stage gingivitis through to more advanced periodontal concerns.

**[Mouthguards](/services/general-preventive/mouthguards/)**
Custom-fitted mouthguards for sport and night guards for teeth grinding, made to fit your teeth precisely for better comfort and protection.

## Why Preventive Care Matters

Preventive dentistry isn't just about avoiding pain — it's about protecting your long-term oral health and reducing the likelihood of needing more extensive, invasive treatment later. Small issues caught early, such as the beginnings of decay or early gum inflammation, are often far simpler and more comfortable to address than problems that have been left untreated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How often should I have a check-up and clean?**
Most patients are recommended a check-up every six months, though your dentist may suggest a different interval based on your oral health and individual risk factors.

**Are X-rays needed at every visit?**
Not necessarily — your dentist will recommend X-rays based on your individual needs, dental history and any specific concerns, rather than as a routine part of every visit.

**Is preventive dentistry suitable for children?**
Yes, preventive care including fluoride treatments and fissure sealants is commonly recommended for children as their adult teeth come through, alongside regular check-ups.

**What happens if gum disease is found during a check-up?**
Your dentist will discuss appropriate treatment options with you, ranging from improved home care and professional cleaning to periodontal treatment, depending on severity.

**Can I combine a check-up with other treatments in one visit?**
This depends on your individual treatment plan and the complexity of any additional treatment — your dentist will let you know what can be scheduled together.

**Do you offer mouthguards for both sport and grinding?**
Yes, we offer custom-fitted mouthguards for sport as well as night guards for teeth grinding, tailored to your specific needs.
