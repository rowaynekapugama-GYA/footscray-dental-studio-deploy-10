## H1: All-on-X Dental Implants

**Breadcrumb:** Home > Services > Restorative Dentistry > All-on-X Dental Implants

For patients missing most or all of their teeth in an upper or lower jaw, All-on-X is a full-arch implant solution that uses a small number of strategically placed implants — typically four or more, hence "All-on-X" — to support a complete arch of replacement teeth. This approach is designed to provide a fixed, functional alternative to a removable full denture.

## What's Involved

All-on-X treatment generally includes:

1. **Comprehensive consultation and assessment** — a detailed discussion of your goals, dental history and general health, along with imaging such as an OPG or CT scan to assess jawbone structure.
2. **Treatment planning** — your dentist develops an individualised plan, which may involve collaboration with a specialist for the surgical stages.
3. **Implant placement** — a small number of implants are strategically positioned across the arch to provide stable support for the full-arch restoration.
4. **Healing period** — time is needed for the implants to integrate with the jawbone (osseointegration), during which a temporary restoration may be worn.
5. **Final restoration** — once healing is complete, a custom-made full-arch restoration is fitted and secured to the implants.

Your dentist will explain the specific staging and expected timeframe for your individual treatment plan during your consultation.

## Benefits of All-on-X

- A fixed alternative to a removable full denture, rather than a removable one
- Uses a smaller number of implants to support a full arch, compared with replacing every tooth individually
- Designed to restore function for eating and speaking, along with appearance
- Can help support facial structure by providing stability similar to natural teeth

## Who It's Suitable For

All-on-X may be an option for patients missing most or all of their teeth in one or both jaws, including those currently wearing a full denture who are looking for a fixed alternative. Suitability depends on individual factors including bone structure, gum health, and general health, all of which your dentist will assess during a comprehensive consultation. Where bone volume is insufficient, your dentist will discuss what options may be available.

## What to Expect During Recovery

As with individual implants, some swelling or discomfort is common following surgical placement, and your dentist will provide guidance on managing this. A healing period is required before your final full-arch restoration is fitted, during which a temporary restoration may be worn to support function and appearance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does "All-on-X" mean?**
"All-on-X" refers to a full arch of replacement teeth supported by a small number of implants — the "X" reflects the number of implants used, typically four or more, depending on your individual treatment plan.

**How is All-on-X different from individual dental implants?**
Individual implants typically replace one or a few missing teeth, while All-on-X uses a small number of implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth for patients missing most or all of their teeth.

**Am I a suitable candidate for All-on-X?**
Suitability depends on factors such as bone density, gum health, and general health — this is assessed during a comprehensive consultation, which is complimentary at Footscray Dental Studio.

**Is All-on-X treatment painful?**
The surgical placement is carried out under anaesthetic, and your dentist will discuss pain management for the recovery period, which varies by individual.

**How long does the All-on-X process take from start to finish?**
This varies depending on individual healing time and treatment planning — your dentist will provide an estimated timeline during your consultation.

**Can I switch from dentures to All-on-X?**
Many patients considering All-on-X are current denture wearers looking for a fixed alternative — your dentist can discuss whether this is a suitable option for your situation.
