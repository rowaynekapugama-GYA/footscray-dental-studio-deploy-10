Fluoride is a naturally occurring mineral that helps strengthen tooth enamel and supports resistance to acid attacks from plaque bacteria and sugars — one of the main contributors to tooth decay. While fluoride toothpaste provides everyday protection, professional fluoride treatments deliver a more concentrated application, offering an added layer of preventive care for patients at higher risk of decay.

At Footscray Dental Studio, we offer professional fluoride treatments as part of our preventive dentistry services, tailored to the needs of each patient.

## What's Involved

A professional fluoride treatment typically involves:

1. **Assessment** — your dentist reviews your oral health and decay risk to determine whether fluoride treatment is appropriate.
2. **Preparation** — your teeth are usually cleaned beforehand, often as part of your regular check-up and clean.
3. **Application** — a concentrated fluoride gel, foam or varnish is applied directly to the teeth.
4. **Aftercare guidance** — your dentist will let you know if there's a recommended waiting period before eating or drinking.

The process is quick, straightforward and generally well tolerated by both children and adults.

## Benefits of Fluoride Treatment

- Helps strengthen tooth enamel against decay
- Can help reduce sensitivity in some patients
- Supports children's developing teeth as they emerge
- A simple, non-invasive addition to routine preventive care
- May help reduce the risk of future cavities, particularly in higher-risk patients

## Who May Benefit Most

Fluoride treatments may be particularly recommended for:

- Children as their teeth develop
- Patients with a history of frequent cavities
- Patients with dry mouth (reduced saliva flow)
- Patients undergoing orthodontic treatment, where plaque control can be more difficult
- Patients with early signs of enamel demineralisation

## Who It's Suitable For

Fluoride treatments may be recommended for children as their teeth develop, as well as adults with a higher risk of tooth decay. Your dentist will advise whether fluoride treatment is appropriate for you as part of your individual care plan.

## What to Expect Afterwards

Your dentist may recommend avoiding eating or drinking for a short period following treatment to allow the fluoride to be fully absorbed — they'll let you know what applies to your specific treatment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is fluoride treatment only for children?**
No, fluoride treatments can also be recommended for adults, particularly those with a higher risk of tooth decay, dry mouth, or sensitivity.

**How often is fluoride treatment recommended?**
This varies by individual — your dentist will advise a schedule based on your oral health needs and risk factors.

**Does fluoride treatment hurt?**
No, it's a quick, non-invasive procedure typically applied during a routine check-up, with no discomfort involved.

**Can I eat or drink straight after treatment?**
Your dentist will let you know if there's a recommended waiting period after your specific treatment to allow it to take full effect.

**Is fluoride treatment covered by health funds?**
Coverage depends on your individual policy — check with your health fund or ask our team when you book.

**Can fluoride treatment be combined with a check-up and clean?**
Yes, it's commonly provided as part of the same visit as your regular check-up and clean for convenience.
