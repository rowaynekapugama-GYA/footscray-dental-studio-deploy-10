Our Services

General & Preventive Dentistry

Good oral health starts with prevention. Our general dentistry services are focused on maintaining healthy teeth and gums, identifying potential issues early, and helping you avoid more complex — and often more costly — treatment down the track.

Book a Check-Up
Overview

What to Expect at Footscray Dental Studio

We recommend a check-up and clean every six months for most patients, though your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your individual needs and risk factors.

At Footscray Dental Studio, our general and preventive services are supported by modern diagnostic technology, including digital X-rays, OPG imaging and intraoral cameras, giving your dentist a clear, detailed picture of your oral health at every visit.

Every general and preventive visit begins with a thorough assessment of your teeth, gums and overall oral health. Your dentist will explain any findings clearly, using diagnostic images where relevant, and talk you through recommended next steps without pressure. Whether you're due for a routine clean or need a specific concern investigated, our approach is the same: clear communication, thorough care, and a comfortable experience.

Our General & Preventive Services

Check-ups & Cleans

Routine examinations and professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, identify early signs of decay or gum disease, and keep your smile in good health. Recommended every six months for most patients.

Learn More

Digital X-Rays / Diagnostics

Detailed, low-radiation imaging — including bitewing, periapical and OPG panoramic X-rays — to support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Learn More

Fluoride Treatments

Professional-strength fluoride application to help strengthen tooth enamel and reduce the risk of decay, suitable for both children and adults.

Learn More

Fissure Sealants

A protective coating applied to the grooved chewing surfaces of back teeth, commonly recommended for children as their adult molars emerge.

Learn More

Gum Disease / Periodontal Care

Assessment and treatment to support healthy gums, from early-stage gingivitis through to more advanced periodontal concerns.

Learn More

Mouthguards

Custom-fitted mouthguards for sport and night guards for teeth grinding, made to fit your teeth precisely for better comfort and protection.

Why Preventive Care Matters

Preventive dentistry isn't just about avoiding pain — it's about protecting your long-term oral health and reducing the likelihood of needing more extensive, invasive treatment later. Small issues caught early, such as the beginnings of decay or early gum inflammation, are often far simpler and more comfortable to address than problems that have been left untreated.
