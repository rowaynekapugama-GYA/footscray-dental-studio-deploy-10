Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry covers a range of treatments designed to improve the appearance of your teeth and smile.

Book a Cosmetic Consultation
Overview

What to Expect at Footscray Dental Studio

At Footscray Dental Studio, every cosmetic treatment begins with a thorough consultation, where your dentist assesses your oral health, discusses your goals, and talks you through the options genuinely suited to your individual situation. Results vary from patient to patient, and your dentist will always give you a realistic picture of what a treatment can achieve before you proceed.

Cosmetic treatment at our practice starts with understanding what you'd like to achieve and why. Your dentist will examine your teeth and gums, discuss suitable options, and explain the process, timeframe and any considerations involved — so you can make an informed decision with no pressure to proceed on the day. Where multiple visits are required, your dentist will map out a clear plan from the outset.

Composite Bonding

A tooth-coloured resin material applied and shaped to address minor imperfections, often completed in a single visit.

Smile Makeovers / Full Mouth Planning

A comprehensive, consultation-led approach for patients considering multiple cosmetic or restorative treatments together as part of a broader plan.

A Consultation-First Approach

Because cosmetic outcomes depend heavily on each patient's individual teeth, gums and goals, we don't offer generic cosmetic packages without an assessment first. Your consultation is the starting point for any cosmetic treatment, ensuring the approach recommended is genuinely appropriate for you.
