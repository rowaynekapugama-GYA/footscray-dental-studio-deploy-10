### What "reframe" means in practice

Six posts cover treatments Footscray Dental Studio no longer provides. In each one:

- The slug, primary keyword and informational value are unchanged, so the ranking signals carry across.
- Any wording suggesting the practice performs the treatment has been removed. Procedures are now described generically: "a dentist or oral surgeon may recommend", "is typically performed by".
- Calls to action point only at what the practice does offer: a check-up or the $135 New Patient Exam & Clean for assessment, emergency care for pain, periodontal care for gums, and for aligner topics the complimentary clear aligner consultation.
- No referral arrangements have been invented. The strongest statement is that your dentist can discuss the appropriate next steps.

## 6. Go-live checklist

**Before switching the old domain**

1. Run `./fetch-blog-images.sh` while ezydentalgroup.com.au is still serving, then rebuild with `cd build && python3 blog.py`.
2. Redeploy the site from the zip so the blog, sitemap and `vercel.json` go live on footscraydentalstudio.com.au.
3. Spot-check five migrated posts on the live domain, including one reframed post.

**Switching the domain**

4. In Vercel, add both `ezydentalgroup.com.au` and `www.ezydentalgroup.com.au` as domains on this project.
5. Point the old domain's DNS at Vercel. The host-based rules in `vercel.json` then serve the 301s automatically. If the old domain stays on its current WordPress host instead, install `old-site.htaccess` or import `rank-math-redirections.csv`.
6. Confirm the redirects are live: test a post (`ezydentalgroup.com.au/tooth-pain-relief/`), a service page (`/services/dental-veneers/`), a dropped service (`/services/invisalign/`), a category (`/category/general-dentistry/`) and pagination (`/blog/2/`). Each should return a single 301 to a 200.

**Search Console**

7. Verify both domains in Google Search Console.
8. Run the **Change of Address** tool from the old property to the new one. This is the single most important step for transferring rankings.
9. Submit `https://www.footscraydentalstudio.com.au/sitemap.xml` on the new property.
10. Keep the old property in Search Console so you can watch its coverage drain across to the new one.

**Elsewhere**

11. Update the Google Business Profile: rename from Ezy Dental Group to Footscray Dental Studio and change the website link. The profile keeps its reviews and history through a rename, which is why renaming beats creating a new listing.
12. Update Facebook and Instagram profile names, bios and website links.
13. Update any directory listings, health fund provider listings and HICAPS records with the new name and URL.

**Aftercare**

14. Keep ezydentalgroup.com.au registered and redirecting for at least 12 months. Two to three years is safer.
15. Watch Search Console for 404s and ranking movement for 4 to 8 weeks. A temporary dip in the first fortnight is normal; the concern is anything still depressed after six weeks.
16. Re-run `python3 build/verify_migration.py` after any future content change to confirm nothing regressed.
