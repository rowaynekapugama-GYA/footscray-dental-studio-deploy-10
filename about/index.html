About us

Our Story

Welcome to Footscray Dental Studio — a fresh, modern chapter for a practice with a strong foundation in the local community. The practice is now under new management, building on the foundations of EZY Dental Group while bringing fresh leadership, experience and a genuine commitment to making every patient's dental experience comfortable, personalised and positive.

Our team has been providing dental services to the local community for many years, with a strong focus on quality care, patient relationships and positive dental experiences. Together, our practitioners bring more than 20 years of combined experience across general and comprehensive dental services.

We believe great dentistry is about more than treating teeth — it's about getting to know our patients, understanding their individual needs, and helping them feel comfortable and confident in their care. As your local dental studio, we're proud to be part of the Footscray community and look forward to caring for generations of patients and families.

Whether you're visiting for a routine check-up, preventive care, or more comprehensive treatment, our team is here to provide professional care with a warm, personalised approach.

Formerly Ezy Dental Group. Footscray Dental Studio is the same practice, at the same address on Barkly St, with many of the same familiar faces. The name and leadership have changed; our commitment to the Footscray community has not.

Our philosophy

Our Philosophy

Our philosophy is simple — we want every patient to feel comfortable, respected and genuinely cared for. We take the time to listen, understand each patient's needs, and provide clear, honest advice so they can make informed decisions about their dental health.

We believe dentistry should be personal, preventive and focused on long-term wellbeing. Our approach combines clinical experience and modern dentistry with a warm, approachable environment where patients feel at ease.

We're committed to building lasting relationships with our patients and families, rather than simply providing treatment. For us, success is seeing our patients leave feeling confident about their oral health, knowing they have a dental team they can trust. As a local practice, we're proud to be part of the Footscray community and look forward to caring for our patients for many years to come.

Modern technology

A Modern, Technology-Led Studio

As part of the transition to Footscray Dental Studio, we've introduced a range of modern dental equipment and digital technologies to enhance patient care, diagnostic accuracy and overall experience, including:

Intraoral cameras

detailed, real-time images of your teeth and gums, helping you better understand your own oral health

Digital X-rays

efficient, high-quality diagnostic imaging with improved patient convenience and lower radiation exposure

Digital dental scanner

accurate digital impressions, more comfortable than traditional putty-based methods

Advanced root canal treatment technology

supporting precise, efficient endodontic care

OPG (Orthopantomogram) imaging

comprehensive panoramic views of teeth, jaws and surrounding structures

Smile photography and digital documentation

a dedicated studio-style setup to capture high-quality images and monitor treatment progress over time

These upgrades are designed to support a modern, technology-enabled dental studio, while maintaining the warm and welcoming environment our community knows.

Familiar faces, new expertise

Continuity, With Fresh Expertise

We highly value the contribution and experience of every member of our existing team. As part of the transition to new leadership, several of our existing practitioners and support staff continue with the practice, providing continuity and familiarity for our patients.

At the same time, we've welcomed additional experienced practitioners to the team, strengthening our clinical capabilities and broadening the range of services available — so we can better support the diverse dental needs of the local community. Our focus is on retaining the strengths and relationships that already exist within the practice, while introducing new expertise, ideas and capabilities under new leadership.
